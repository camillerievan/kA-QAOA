3-uniform cyclic sign-alternating hypergraphs,
closing the chain of interactions, so every vertex
sits in three hyperedges.

cyclic.csv  1063 rows, one per (graph, depth, variant)
graphs      12, n in {4, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 15}
depths      1 to 6
variants    am, ka, ma, sa

The family of each graph is taken from tb_Graph.graph_name,
and for the unnamed duplicates at n = 4 from the term count.

AR is <C>/C_opt, with c_opt recomputed from the problem
definition; the database stores the attained expectation but
no optimum.
prob_opt_pct is the probability of measuring an optimal
assignment, as recorded.
