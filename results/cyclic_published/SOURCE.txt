3-uniform cyclic sign-alternating hypergraphs,
closing the chain of interactions, so every vertex
sits in three hyperedges.

cyclic.csv  1296 rows, one per (graph, depth, variant, initialisation)
graphs      14 over 12 sizes, n in {4, ..., 15};
            three distinct graphs at n = 4, one at every other size
depths      1 to 5 at every size; 6 at every size except 10, 14, 15
variants    am, ka, ma, sa
optimiser   BFGS, one run per variant, initialisation and depth;
            the initialisation is not recorded per row

Sources, all from the same ex09 code and settings:
  n = 4..9, 11..13   May 2024 runs, as exported from the results
                     database
  n = 10, 14         July 2024 runs (instances 25, 26), p = 1..5,
                     the first recorded run of each configuration
  n = 15             August 2024 rerun (instance 27), p = 1..5,
                     the first recorded run of each configuration

The May 2024 export had filed one n = 15 graph as cyclic.  It has
41 cost terms, which is the path family (cyclic n = 15 has 46), so
its rows are in path_published/ instead.

The family of each graph is taken from its cost-term count.

AR is <C>/C_opt, with c_opt recomputed from the problem
definition; the stored rows carry the attained expectation but
no optimum.
prob_opt_pct is the probability of measuring an optimal
assignment, as recorded.
