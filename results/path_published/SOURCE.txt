3-uniform path sign-alternating hypergraphs,
leaving the chain open, so the two vertices at each end
sit in fewer hyperedges than the rest.

cyclic.csv  1308 rows, one per (graph, depth, variant, initialisation)
graphs      14 over 12 sizes, n in {4, ..., 15};
            three distinct graphs at n = 4, one at every other size
depths      1 to 5 at every size; 6 at every size except 10, and
            only in part at 14 and 15
variants    am, ka, ma, sa
optimiser   BFGS, one run per variant, initialisation and depth;
            the initialisation is not recorded per row

Sources, all from the same ex09 code and settings:
  n = 4..9, 11..13   May 2024 runs, as exported from the results
                     database
  n = 14, 15         May 2024 runs where they exist (instances 23, 24;
                     the n = 15 graph was filed as cyclic in that
                     export and is moved here), completed to
                     p = 1..5 from the July 2024 (n = 14) and
                     August 2024 (n = 15) runs
  n = 10             July 2024 runs (instance 28), p = 1..5
Where July/August runs are used, the first recorded run of each
configuration is kept.

The family of each graph is taken from its cost-term count.

AR is <C>/C_opt, with c_opt recomputed from the problem
definition; the stored rows carry the attained expectation but
no optimum.
prob_opt_pct is the probability of measuring an optimal
assignment, as recorded.
